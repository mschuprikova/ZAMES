**Z**onal **A**nalysis of **M**utations & **E**valuation of **S**anger
--- **ZAMES 1.0** --- is a web application designed for the quantitative analysis of Sanger 
sequencing chromatograms to estimate mutation frequencies in DNA samples. By 
analyzing nucleotide signal intensities within a user-defined target region, 
ZAMES enables the detection of minor nucleotide variants and quantifies them 
relative to the corresponding reference signal.

The application is intended for researchers studying DNA polymerase fidelity, 
DNA replication accuracy, and the molecular mechanisms of mutagenesis.

**ZAMES 1.0** is still in development, so please be kind to it!
